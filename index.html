About Bold Text

What is bold text? And why do I need a generator?

Bold text is a typographic style where characters appear thicker and darker than regular text, making them stand out on a page or screen. Traditionally, bold is applied with HTML <b> tags or a toolbar button — but most social media platforms and messaging apps strip those styles away the moment you paste.

That’s where a bold text generator comes in. By using Unicode mathematical bold characters, you produce text that looks bold everywhere — from Instagram bios to Twitter posts to email subject lines — without relying on any formatting support from the platform.

Technology

Unicode & Unicode Bold Characters

Unicode is the universal encoding standard assigning a unique number to every character across 150+ writing systems. Because every modern OS, browser, and platform supports Unicode, a character typed anywhere displays identically everywhere.

Within Unicode, the Mathematical Alphanumeric Symbols block (U+1D400–U+1D7FF) contains dedicated bold versions of every Latin letter and digit. 𝐀 is a completely different code point from A — so when you paste it, the boldness is intrinsic to the character itself, not styling layered on top. That’s why it works in plain-text fields where HTML bold cannot.
